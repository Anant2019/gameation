Alchemix Lab — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 08/10/2026 · Developer: code_infi · Contact: mail2codeinfi@gmail.com

Summary

Alchemix Lab is a virtual chemistry lab. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no ads. It does not collect, send or share any personal data. Your progress is saved only on your device.

Information the app stores on your device

The app saves your game stars, completed school experiments, quiz scores and the reactions you have discovered in the app's local storage on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services, and it does not request any special permissions such as location, camera, microphone or contacts. It works fully offline.

Children

Alchemix Lab is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Because no personal data leaves your device, there is nothing for us to store or protect on our side. We do not operate any servers that receive data from the app.

Changes

If a future version adds features that use data (for example, advertising), we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
