📚 HOW TO PLAY

STEP 1 OF 3

🔍 SEARCH ROOMS FOR CLUES

  • Click any room on the map to enter it
  • Some rooms are locked and require a key found elsewhere!
  • Click glowing gold circles on objects
  • Each clue tells you the Thief, Tool, or Location
  • Collect 4+ clues to unlock accusation
STEP 2 OF 3

🗣 INTERROGATE — CATCH LIARS!

  • Click a suspect → ask all 3 questions
  • Click Present Evidence to show them clues!
  • Portrait glows RED + shakes = LYING!
  • Guilty ones say things like "I had to act"
  • Interview 2+ suspects to unlock accusation
STEP 3 OF 3

⚖ MAKE YOUR ACCUSATION!

  • Click ⚖ Make Accusation on the map
  • Open 📓 Evidence Journal to review first
  • Pick THIEF — whose name appears in clues?
  • Pick TOOL — what object was used?
  • Pick LOCATION — which room had the evidence?
  • All 3 correct = YOU WIN! 🏆
