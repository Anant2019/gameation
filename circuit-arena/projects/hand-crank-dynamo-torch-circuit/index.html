How it works

  1. The dynamo makes a voltage when you turn it.
  2. A switch turns the torch on and off.
  3. A resistor protects the LED from too much current.

  • Tap the switch to turn it ON.
  • A small DC motor works as a dynamo when you spin it by hand.
