9 V to 5 V Regulator using 7805

Beginner6 min buildParts about ₹293

Most modules and Arduino projects need a steady 5 V. A 7805 regulator turns a 9 V battery (or 7–12 V adapter) into a clean 5 V.

How it works

  1. The battery connects to the 7805 IN pin and ground.
  2. The 7805 keeps its OUT pin at 5 V whatever the input voltage between about 7 and 20 V.
  3. An LED with a resistor shows the output is on, and a voltmeter shows 5 V.

Tips for building the real thing

  • The voltmeter across OUT and GND should read 5.00 V.
  • Add a 0.33 µF capacitor on the input and 0.1 µF on the output for stability.
  • The 7805 gets hot above about 500 mA: use a heat sink.

FAQ

What are the pins of a 7805?

Looking at the front: 1 = input, 2 = ground, 3 = output (5 V).

Is a 7805 efficient?

No, the extra voltage becomes heat. For batteries, a buck converter wastes much less.
