How it works

  1. The solar panel makes about 6 V in sunlight.
  2. A diode stops current flowing back into the panel.
  3. A resistor limits the LED current so it does not burn out.

Tips for building the real thing

  • The diode band (−) faces the resistor.
  • Add a rechargeable cell and an LDR switch to make a real light that turns on at night.

FAQ

Why is a diode used with a solar panel?

It blocks reverse current, for example from a battery back into the panel at night.
