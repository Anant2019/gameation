Circuit Arena Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Circuit Arena is an offline electronics simulation game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Android app.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for an account, does not collect personal information, and has no analytics, advertising or tracking. It does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything off your device.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, best scores, saved builds and settings (sound, vibration, tips) are saved only inside the app on your own device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them. We cannot see or recover this data.

"Cloud" and "Wi-Fi" features in the game

The IoT dashboard, ESP32 Wi-Fi and cloud features are simulations that run entirely on your device. No real network connection is made.

Web version

The browser version at calmtype.life works the same way: your saves stay in your own browser. To show its fonts it loads them from Google Fonts, so Google receives the usual request details (such as your IP address) when the page opens. The Android app has the fonts built in and makes no network requests.

AI features (web version, optional)

The AI builder and the Circuit Doctor only work if you add your own API key from Google (Gemini) or Anthropic (Claude). Your key is saved only on your device. When you use these features, your device sends the text you type and a description of the circuit on your bench (part types, values, wires, readings and any chip code) directly to the AI company you chose, under your own account and its terms. Nothing passes through our servers, and we never see your key or your requests. Do not type personal information into these boxes. You can remove your key at any time in the AI window. The rest of the game works without the AI and sends nothing.

Permissions

The app uses the vibration permission only for short haptic feedback when you tap. You can switch this off in the app's Home settings.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
