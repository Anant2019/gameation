Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026 · Site: calmtype.life · Contact: mail2codeinfi@gmail.com

Summary

CalmType is a collection of free games that run in your browser. There are no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email or any other personal details to play.

What the games save on your device

Games save things like your scores, in-game money, settings and progress in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

Visitor statistics

We may count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It tells us things like how many people visited a page and which link they came from.

Advertising

Some games may show ads, for example an optional video you can watch for an in-game reward, or a short ad between rounds. Ads are served by Google (AdSense and its H5 games ads) or, when you play on a game portal, by that portal's ad partners.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Read how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Sharing

Share buttons open WhatsApp or your phone's share menu with a ready message. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Kids Zone games show no ads.

Changes and contact
