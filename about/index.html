About CalmType

CalmType is a small independent game maker, building free games that run straight in your browser. No downloads, no accounts: open a link and play, on a phone or a laptop.

Our games

  • Rickshaw Rush: drive a 3D auto rickshaw through Pune, pick up passengers and escape the police.
  • Typing practice, puzzle and action games, and a Kids Zone with games for young children.

See them all on the home page.
